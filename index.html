Our school

A school at the heart of its community.

At Sunnyside, learning is about helping every child build knowledge, confidence, friendships and a sense of what they can contribute. We work in partnership with families so children feel safe, supported and ready to learn.

Our school is part of the Smithycroft Learning Community and includes a dedicated Language and Communication Resource. Together, we celebrate progress, encourage curiosity and help pupils take an active role in school and community life.

“Children do their best learning when they feel that they belong, that their ideas matter and that the adults around them believe in them.”

Learning at Sunnyside

Curious minds. Confident voices.

Learning follows Scotland’s Curriculum for Excellence, helping children grow across four connected capacities.

Successful learners

Children develop enthusiasm, resilience and the skills to think, question and apply what they know.

Confident individuals

Pupils learn to express themselves, make informed choices and recognise their own strengths.

Responsible citizens

Children explore their place in the local community and wider world with care and respect.

Effective contributors

Pupils collaborate, solve problems, share ideas and learn how their actions can make a difference.

Strong foundations, meaningful experiences

Literacy, numeracy and health and wellbeing sit at the heart of learning. Children also explore sciences, social studies, technologies, expressive arts, languages and religious and moral education through connected experiences.

Common questions

A quick answer may be all you need.

Who should I contact if my child is absent?
Please call the school office on 0141 774 5777 as early as you can. Leave your child’s name, class and the reason for their absence.
How do I apply for a Primary 1 place?
Glasgow City Council manages Primary 1 registration online. Start with the council’s enrolment guidance. Families moving into the area can also phone the school for help.
Where can I find information about additional support?
Sunnyside has a Language and Communication Resource. You can read the Glasgow City Council overview, or contact the school to discuss your child’s needs and the appropriate referral route.
Can I get this information in another language or format?
Yes. Contact the school office by phone or email and tell us the language or accessible format you need. We will help you find the right support.
Where do I find policies and inspection information?
The Education Scotland inspection page holds inspection information. For a current school policy or document, contact the office and we will send the correct version.
